Mod Details:

AdsMod adds three billboards to the game, giving players the ability to display the image of their choice using the printer system.

- Large Format Billboard
- Urban Billboard
- Mobile Poster

Usage:

Usage is very simple. Each item can be crafted from the mechanics table. You can also find each item in the Ecopedia.

Installation:

Place the "AdsMod_YOURLANGUAGE" folder in the "/Mods/UserCode" folder and also add the "Assets" folder to "/Mods/UserCode".
